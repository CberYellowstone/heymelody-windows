EXTRACTED EARPHONE RESOURCES

This distribution contains device configuration and artwork supplied for this
project's compatibility work. Current bundled assets originate from HeyMelody
17.6.5 (com.heytap.headset), verified device artwork packages delivered by the
vendor service, and the separately supplied X4 images.
OPPO, OnePlus, realme, their logos, product artwork and other vendor materials
remain the property of their respective rights holders. This project's source
license does not relicense those materials; no open-source asset license is
claimed here. The two X4 PNGs were separately supplied for this project.

Current left-ear, right-ear and charging-case interface pictograms are adapted
from the 14x14 melody_ui_battery_device_left, melody_ui_battery_device_right
and melody_ui_battery_device_box vectors verified in HeyMelody 17.6.5. These
restore the historical circular L/R and matching case shapes while using
HeyMelody provenance. Their original resource paths and hashes, including
preserved earlier 19x16 research, are recorded in
docs/research/battery-device-icons.md. These interface geometries are separate
from the ResourceBundles device artwork.

Earlier releases embedded monochrome outlines copied from Wireless Earphones
17.6.3 (com.oplus.melody). That component's exclusive configuration and artwork
remain outside the current application. Historical attribution and preserved
original evidence remain in the source archive; this distribution does not
claim to relicense that material.

From 0.8.13 the Host also embeds a PCM16 conversion of the official Golden
Hearing module-2 audition music, res/raw/hearing_enhancement_audition.mp3,
from android_all_all_all_2.zip delivered by the vendor service. It remains
vendor material under the same rights notice. Hearing-Audition-SOURCE.json
records the source URL, original archive/audio hashes and converted asset
hash. FFmpeg was used only as an offline conversion tool and is not bundled.

ResourceBundles/provenance.json identifies the APK versions and SHA256 values.
ResourceBundles/artwork.json identifies exact product/color association,
file paths and hashes. ResourceBundles/supplemental.json records the HeyMelody
scanning image. Installed offline assets are kept separate from the
user's downloaded cache. Original research inputs are not required at runtime.

The independent desktop Lottie renderer uses the separately licensed
SkiaSharp.Skottie dependency. Avalonia.Labs source code is not included.
